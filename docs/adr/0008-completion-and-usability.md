# ADR 0008 — Completing the remaining modules and simplifying every workspace

Status: accepted · 2026-11-26

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One home screen for every role, driven by live work counts** (`GET /home`). Each count is computed with the same permission and branch rules as the screen it links to, returns numbers only, and is shown under "Needs your attention" only when it is above zero. Today's figures and up to six shortcuts depend on the role. The old table of module codes and access letters is gone. | Spec §10.2 role-aware workspaces; users asked for fewer, clearer screens. |
| 2 | **The sidebar lists screens, not modules.** Several modules that open the same screen appear once (wards, beds, nursing). Worklists (laboratory, imaging, ECG, pharmacy) are shown only to department staff who can work them; clinicians who order work do it from the visit. Page headers carry a title and one short line; spec references and long explanations were removed from the interface. | Remove anything that confuses the user (product owner request). |
| 3 | **Quality, infection control & incidents (§5.28).** Any member of staff reports an incident; the content is visible to the quality team and the reporter, the reporter's name only to quality leads. Reported → Triaged → Investigating → Closed; a serious incident needs a corrective action, and the reporter cannot close their own report. Infection cases move Suspected → Confirmed / Ruled out → Closed. Trends are aggregate counts only. | Spec §5.28 "confidential incident permissions; trend aggregation". |
| 4 | **Telemedicine (§5.27).** A video visit cannot start until the patient's consent is recorded; consent to recording is stored as the patient's answer and Fahan never records calls. Starting the call opens an ordinary outpatient visit, so notes, orders and diagnoses join the record. Rooms live on an external video service (`TELE_BASE_URL`) under an unguessable name; production should point this at a hospital-controlled service. | Spec §5.27 "do not record calls without approved consent"; "visit documentation joins longitudinal EHR". |
| 5 | **Patient portal (§5.26).** Portal accounts (patient, caregiver) are fenced off from the whole staff API and reach only the records linked to them. Caregiver (proxy) access needs a records officer other than the person who asked. Results appear only after a clinician on the visit shares them. Online booking uses free clinic slots at least an hour ahead (three online bookings at a time) and cancellations up to two hours before. Messages go to the front desk. | Spec §5.26 "patient cannot see another patient's data"; "clinician-reviewed release"; "proxy rules". |
| 6 | **Intensive care (§5.10).** A critical-care stay belongs to an active admission (one open stay per admission). Devices are labelled on the stay and can serve one patient at a time; readings keep their source and device id. The daily review records acuity and ventilation. Nothing treats the patient automatically. | Spec §5.10 "device feeds must be labeled; no unvalidated auto-treatment". |
| 7 | **CSSD (§5.24).** Sterilisation loads run only in a working steriliser; both chemical and biological indicators must pass before release; a failed load is never released; a released load can be recalled by the biomedical lead with its sets listed. | Spec §5.24 "sterilization, safety certification tracked". |
| 8 | **Super Admin console (§3.3).** Hospital profile; a module switchboard (administration, security and patient registration can never be switched off) enforced by the API on the very next request; authenticator resets with dual control; fresh invitation links; roles explained in plain words with a preview of what a person will be able to do. | Spec §3.3 "module and feature switchboard", "MFA resets with dual control", "effective permission preview". |

## Still open
- A general workflow engine (event triggers, timers, notifications beyond the home counts) — forms keep their approval rules.
- Integrations that need partner systems: HL7 v2, DICOMweb, LIS analysers, live payment providers, SMS/e-mail notifications.
- People work: clinical UAT, independent penetration test, legal and clinical-governance review, native-speaker review of Somali text.
